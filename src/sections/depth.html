API-first

The core data operations are API-first. If you can publish, query, or evaluate data in the interface, you can do it over the API.

Your data stays yours

Pull all of it out over the API into your own stack, Snowflake, Databricks, a warehouse, or flat files. Nothing is held in a proprietary format on the way out.

Security posture

Role-based access through groups and members, and an activity log of who did what. SSO and SAML, audit-log retention, data-residency options, and self-hosted or managed deployment.
