Requirements

  • The section needs position: relative and overflow: clip (or hidden). The outer sphere bleeds off the right edge on desktop, and the section crops it.
  • The canvas is exactly .u-particles-threejs: the spheres are drawn inside that div, with room around them, so they're never cut at its edges.
  • Desktop: the spheres are drawn at the center of .u-particles-threejs, with the outer shell's radius at 38% of the div's side. To move or resize the spheres, move or resize the div.
  • Tablet (768–991 px): same horizontal layout as desktop. The div keeps desktop's proportions (width: 70.3% of the hero, same center), so the spheres scale with the screen; the text stays in the left half (max-width: calc(50% - 24px)).
  • Mobile (767 px and below): the hero stacks. The div is a full-width band, 50% of the screen height (50svh), right under the navbar (--navbar-h: 63 px; use your navbar's real height). The spheres are centered in it and fitted inside it (the preset has settings per breakpoint): as big as the band allows, 8 px inside its edges, so they never reach under the navbar or into the text. The hero's top padding reserves navbar + band + 36 px, and the text follows.
  • Keep the hero content above the effect: position: relative; z-index: 1 on the content wrapper.
  • The navbar is not part of the effect, and needs no attributes.

Behaviour to expect

  • Four dotted shapes in a loop: three nested spheres → meridians → a spiral → a burst. Each holds for 6 s, then morphs into the next over 2 s (the CTA's timing). They're there from the first frame (no entrance), as the CTA.
  • Depth: the spheres turn gently toward the cursor.
  • Hover: dots near the cursor are pushed aside.
  • Buttons: hovering “Book a demo” or “Talk to an engineer” lights all the dots up (mouse only), as “Get started” does on the CTA.
  • Click (desktop): the dots burst outward, then reform. Not on tablet and mobile.
  • Touch (phones, tablets): no reaction to a finger (no hover, no tap, no spin), so swipes always scroll the page.
  • Tablet (768–991 px): the spheres are smaller, so they use fewer, smaller dots (4,500; 3,300 at 879 px and below) to keep desktop's look.
  • 767 px and below: the spheres centered in the band and fitted inside it, with 4,500 dots at 60% size instead of 7,000, rendered at the phone's full resolution.
  • Resizing across 768 px swaps the layouts.

2Mark up each section

Each effect is a component. Every section tab lists its exact attributes.

Structure
[data-particles-component]   the component: its preset, and the scope for its buttons
  [data-particles-wrap]      where the effect goes (optional: defaults to the component)
    div.u-particles-threejs  CREATED BY THE SCRIPT: style this class in Webflow
      <canvas>               created by the script, 100% × 100% of that div
  • data-preset goes on the component (or the wrap, which wins). The look lives in the preset, so no design settings go into Webflow.
  • The hero uses data-how-component and data-how-wrap: same thing, other names. Either pair works on any section.
  • data-particles-excite on a button inside a component lights up that component's effect when hovered or focused.
  • If the div ends up with no size, the console warns you.

Site-wide typography

From the design: headings balance their lines and paragraphs avoid one-word last lines. Add once (site CSS or Head custom code):

CSS
h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6 { text-wrap: balance; }
p { text-wrap: pretty; }

3Check it

  • Custom code doesn't run in the Webflow Designer. Check in Preview or on the published staging site.
  • Console warnings start with [particles] and name the element and the bad attribute or preset.
  • ClarifyParticles.debug() in the console shows the live state.

Built in

  • The engine (~147 kB gzipped, Three.js included) loads after the page's load event, once a particle section is within half a screen. All sections share one WebGL context.
  • Stops completely when no particle section is on screen. prefers-reduced-motion gets a still frame. Without WebGL, the section shows its normal design.
  • No restart on resize, including the mobile address bar.
